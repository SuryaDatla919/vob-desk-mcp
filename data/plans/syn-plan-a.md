# SYN-PLAN-A (synthetic dental plan)

Invented terms for a student demo. They are not based on any real plan. Procedure IDs are invented and are not ADA CDT codes. Each `###` heading is a clause that tools can cite, for example `SYN-PLAN-A §4.2`.

## §1 Eligibility

### §1.1 Coverage period
Coverage is active from the member's effective date through the termination date, inclusive. A member with no termination date stays covered. Benefits are checked for the planned date of service only.

### §1.2 Members and dependents
Each covered person has a member ID. The subscriber's ID ends in -01, and dependents on the same contract end in -02 and up. A request may use the subscriber's ID together with the patient's date of birth.

## §2 Deductible

### §2.1 Deductible amount
Each member has a $50 deductible per calendar year.

### §2.2 Preventive services
The deductible does not apply to preventive services.

## §3 Annual maximum

### §3.1 Annual maximum
Benefits are limited to $1,500 per member per calendar year. All covered services count toward the annual maximum.

## §4 Coinsurance and frequency

### §4.1 Coinsurance by class
The plan pays this share of the allowed amount for covered services: preventive 100%, basic 80%, major 50%, orthodontic 50%. Classes follow the procedure catalog: PREV- IDs are preventive, BASIC- basic, MAJOR- major, ORTHO- orthodontic and COSM- cosmetic. Implants (MAJOR-IMPLANT) are covered as a major service.

### §4.2 Frequency limits
PREV-EXAM: 2 per calendar year. PREV-CLEAN-ADULT: 2 per calendar year. PREV-BITEWING: 1 per calendar year. PREV-FLUORIDE-CHILD: 2 per calendar year. PREV-SEALANT: 1 per 36 months. MAJOR-CROWN: 1 per 60 months. Frequency is counted per member. A calendar-year limit renews on January 1. A month-based limit renews on the same calendar day, the stated number of months after the earlier service.

## §5 Waiting periods

### §5.1 Waiting period for basic services
Basic services are covered beginning 6 months after the member's effective date. Preventive services have no waiting period.

### §5.2 Waiting period for major services
Major services are covered beginning 12 months after the member's effective date.

### §5.3 Waiting period for orthodontic services
Orthodontic services are covered beginning 12 months after the member's effective date.

## §6 Age limits

### §6.1 Fluoride age limit
PREV-FLUORIDE-CHILD is covered for members under age 19 on the date of service.

### §6.2 Sealant age limit
PREV-SEALANT is covered for members under age 16 on the date of service.

### §6.3 Orthodontic age limit
ORTHO-CHILD is covered for members under age 19 on the date of service.

## §7 Exclusions

### §7.1 Cosmetic exclusion
Cosmetic services, including COSM-WHITENING, are not covered.

## §8 Alternate benefit

### §8.1 Least costly alternative
When more than one professionally accepted treatment exists, the plan may base its benefit on the least costly one. This demo does not calculate alternate benefits.

## §9 Predetermination

### §9.1 Predetermination for major services
A predetermination of benefits is recommended before major services begin. A predetermination is an estimate, not a guarantee of payment.

## §10 Coordination of benefits

### §10.1 Other dental coverage
If a member also has other dental coverage, a reviewer must confirm which plan pays first before benefits are quoted.

## §11 Not a guarantee of payment

### §11.1 Benefit information
Benefit information is not a guarantee of payment. Payment depends on eligibility, plan terms and the claim as submitted for the actual date of service.
